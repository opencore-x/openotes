# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, friendly place for everyone.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful.** Treat others the way you'd like to be treated.
- **Be kind and constructive.** Assume good intent and offer helpful feedback.
- **No harassment.** Discrimination, insults, personal attacks, or unwelcome
  behavior of any kind are not tolerated.
- **Be inclusive.** People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening
an issue or contacting the maintainers directly. Reports will be handled
promptly, confidentially, and with care.

Thanks for helping keep our community friendly and welcoming!
